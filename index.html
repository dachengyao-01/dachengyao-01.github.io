About Me

I am a Professor at the Institute of Applied Mathematics, Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research and Cybernetics from the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2010, advised by Hanqin Zhang. Before that, I received my B.S. in Information and Computing Science from Shandong University in 2005.

Research Interests

My research focuses on inventory management, queueing theory, and the application of data-driven methods and machine learning to both fields.

My current theoretical interests include optimal policies for stochastic inventory models, the optimization and approximation of stochastic queueing network models, and minimal representations of phase-type distributions.

My applied work focuses on the use of stochastic models, particularly stochastic queueing networks, in autonomous taxi systems and communication networks.
